            🔒 Your family's privacy
            1. Your family has its own private space. Other families can never see your data.
            2. Everything is encrypted when it travels on the internet (HTTPS) and when it is stored (Google Firebase).
            3. Kids join with a 6-letter code. No email, no phone number, no full name needed.
            4. We never sell your data, never show ads, and never share it with anyone.
            5. The app owner can technically open the database to fix problems. We promise not to look at your family's data unless you ask us to help.
            6. 🤖 AI helper: when you use it, the school work you send goes to Anthropic (the company that makes Claude) only to make the answer. We don't keep these files on our server, and Anthropic does not use them to train its AI.
            7. Inside the family: parents can see the kids' plans and moods. A kid can tick "🔒 Just for me" on My day so the text stays private (the mood still shows).
            8. Want your family's data deleted? Ask us and we will delete all of it.
            9. 📊 We count how many people use the app and which pages they open (no names, no family data), to make the app better.
