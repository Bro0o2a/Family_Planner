🗑️ Delete your StudyCal account

You can delete your family's StudyCal account and all of its data at any time. It is free and takes a minute.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open StudyCal on a parent's phone and sign in with the family's email.
  2. Go to Settings → Family account.
  3. Tap Delete family account.
  4. Type your password and the word DELETE, then tap Delete forever.

Tip: before deleting, you can keep your own copy with Settings → Backup → Save a copy.

What is deleted

  • The family's calendar, activities, routines, points, stars, habits and "My day" notes.
  • School binders, school files and photos, worksheets, explanations and their results.
  • The kids' codes, every family member's sign-in link to the family, and the parent's login.
  • App problem reports and AI answer reports of the family.

Deleting can't be undone. All family devices are signed out.

What is not kept by us: AI requests are never stored on our server. Anonymous usage counts (no names, no family content) may stay in Google Analytics for up to 14 months.

Another parent

A second parent (who joined with a parent code) can delete only their own login the same way. The family's data stays for the parent who created the family.

By email

Can't open the app? Email studycalapp.help@gmail.com from the family's email address with the subject "Delete my StudyCal account". We will delete everything within 30 days and tell you when it is done.
