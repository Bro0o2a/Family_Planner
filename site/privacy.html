🔒 Privacy policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

StudyCal ("we", "the app") is a family planner for parents and their children. This policy explains what data the app collects, why, who helps us run it, and how you can delete it. In short: your family's data is only for your family. We never sell it, never show ads, and never use it to train AI.

Who is responsible

StudyCal is run from Dubai, United Arab Emirates. Contact: studycalapp.help@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Parent account: the parent's email address and password (handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see the password), the family name and the parent's first name.
  • Children: a parent adds each child with a first name (or nickname), a picture or emoji and a color. Optionally the school year and school name. Children join on their own device with a 6-letter code. We do not ask children for an email, phone number, full name or birthday.
  • What the family puts in the app: the calendar and activities, ticks, points and stars, habits, "My day" notes and moods, school subjects, school files and photos, worksheets, explanations and their results.
  • App problems: when the app breaks, a short error message (the error text, the app version, the screen and parent or kid). Never the family's content. Parents can see and clear these in Settings.
  • Reports about AI answers: when someone taps "Report", the reason, an optional short note and which answer it was.
  • Usage counts (parents' devices only): how many people open the app and which pages, through Google Analytics for Firebase, without names or family content. Never on children's devices.

How we use it

  • To run the app for your family: show the same plan on every family device, keep points, make worksheets, and so on.
  • To fix problems and make the app better.
  • We do not use the data for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not build profiles for anyone outside your family.

The AI study helper

The AI helper is off until a parent agrees on a screen that explains this. When the family uses it, the school work you choose (photos, files, answers) and the child's first name, school year and school are sent to Anthropic (the company that makes Claude) through our small server, only to make the answer. Our server does not keep this content; it only counts how many AI answers each family used this month (a number, so the family's plan limit works). Anthropic does not use this data to train its AI. Every AI answer has a "Report" button for answers that are wrong or not right for a child.

Who helps us run StudyCal

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore database, Analytics): stores the family's data and logins. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest.
  • Anthropic: makes the AI answers, only when the family uses the AI helper.
  • Cloudflare: runs our small AI server, which passes the request to Anthropic.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the app's files and this website.

We never share the data with anyone else, unless the law requires it.

Children's privacy

StudyCal is set up and controlled by parents. A parent creates the family, adds the children and decides whether to turn on the AI helper. Children's devices do not send usage counts. Inside the family, parents can see the children's plans and moods; a child can mark a "My day" note as "Just for me" so the text stays private. If you think a child's data was added without a parent's permission, email us and we will delete it.

Who can see the data

Only the members of your family, on their signed-in devices. Other families can never see it. As the app's owner we can technically open the database to fix problems; we will not look at your family's data unless you ask us to help.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

We keep the family's data while the family account exists. A parent can delete the whole family account at any time in the app: Settings → Family account → Delete family account. This deletes all of the family's data, files, worksheets, codes and logins at once. See Delete your account. You can also email us and we will delete it for you within 30 days.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your family's data (parents can also save a copy any time in Settings → Backup), ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email studycalapp.help@gmail.com.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and tell families in the app when the change is important.
